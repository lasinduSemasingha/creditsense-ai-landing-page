Literature Survey

What Prior Research Tells Us

📚

ML in Financial Leasing

Traditional rule-based systems depend on limited financial indicators. Ensemble methods (Random Forest, XGBoost, Gradient Boosting) have outperformed statistical baselines by 15–30% in credit risk prediction tasks.

⚠️

Credit Risk & Default Probability

Static models underperform during economic fluctuations. Behavioral indicators — delayed payments, arrears ratios, repayment consistency — significantly boost PD model accuracy beyond 90% when combined with financial attributes.

📐

IFRS 9 & ECL Estimation

Forward-looking ECL models using Gradient Boosting, CatBoost, and LightGBM outperform traditional fixed-parameter impairment models, especially for skewed leasing data distributions.

💬

AI-Based Customer Interaction

RAG architectures combining vector embeddings and similarity search achieve higher response accuracy than rule-based chatbots. Multimodal capabilities (STT, TTS, OCR) improve accessibility in financial services.

Research Gap

Gaps in Existing Literature

Five critical gaps motivated this research:

🔗

No Integrated Framework

Most studies focus on isolated single-task ML applications rather than a unified financial leasing system.

📡

Limited Behavioral Data Use

Default and credit risk models lack adequate incorporation of behavioral indicators and real-time processing capabilities.

📊

Weak ECL Ensemble Modeling

Impairment and ECL research lacks robust ensemble validation on large-scale vehicle leasing datasets.

🔄

Reactive Performance Monitoring

Existing performance monitoring solutions are largely reactive without scalable deployment mechanisms.

🌐

No Leasing-Specific Chatbot

Very few AI conversational systems are tailored specifically for financial leasing workflows and multimodal customer engagement.

Research Problem

The Core Problem

Financial leasing institutions face escalating risks from customer defaults, non-performing loans (NPLs), inefficient branch operations, regulatory compliance demands under IFRS 9, and rising customer service expectations — yet existing data systems and analytical approaches are fragmented, reactive, and insufficient for proactive, intelligent risk management at scale.

Methodology

How We Built It

📋 Data Collection

532,482 vehicle leasing records from a Sri Lankan financial institution (2025–2026), 30 primary features, covering demographics, financial attributes, behavioral patterns, collateral, and transactions. Subset of 188,866 records used for default model training.

🔧 Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

Median imputation for <5% missing values, 'Unknown' for categoricals. Engineered: LTV Ratio, Payment Coverage Ratio, Arrears Severity, log transforms (facility amount, net rental), polynomial features, composite Risk Score.

🤖 Model Development

Evaluated Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost (default), LightGBM, CatBoost (ECL). Grid Search with 5-fold cross-validation. 80/20 stratified splits. class-imbalance handled via scale_pos_weight and undersampling.

🚀 Deployment Architecture

Microservices via FastAPI with joblib serialization, Pydantic validation, Supabase PostgreSQL, FAISS vector store, sentence-transformers, and Next.js 14 frontend dashboards.
